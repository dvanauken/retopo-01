      Choose Draw (or press G), then hold the mouse button and drag on either mesh. Blue guides appear as you draw. Guides in both views attach to the original source surface. Generate retopology to build a new polygon mesh around those curves. Rotate the model to reach another face; a stroke across the hole is split rather than bridged through empty space.

      A closed guide can produce a continuous loop. Crossing guides set local flow in two directions. The hard-surface example preserves its sharp features and its through-hole. “Sharp edges” controls their display; it does not remove preservation.

      The hollow hexagon is from Kenney’s CC0 Prototype Kit. The terrain and curved-cylinder examples come from our research fixtures. All calculations run locally. The smooth examples take longer than the planar solid.
